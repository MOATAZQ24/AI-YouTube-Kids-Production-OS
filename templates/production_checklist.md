# Production Quality Checklist

Ensure every video meets these standards before publishing.

## Visual Checks
- [ ] Character consistency maintained (colors, clothing, proportions).
- [ ] No visual glitches or AI artifacts (extra limbs, distorted faces).
- [ ] Colors are vibrant and high-contrast.
- [ ] Transitions are smooth (1.5s recommended).

## Audio Checks
- [ ] Voiceover is clear and enunciation is perfect.
- [ ] BGM does not overpower the narration.
- [ ] SFX are synced with visual actions.
- [ ] Tone matches the Brand Bible.

## Safety & Compliance
- [ ] No dangerous acts or harmful themes.
- [ ] Content is "Made for Kids" compliant.
- [ ] Educational goal is clearly stated or demonstrated.

## SEO & Meta
- [ ] Title includes a curiosity hook.
- [ ] Thumbnail is clear and high-contrast.
- [ ] Description includes educational summary and hashtags.
