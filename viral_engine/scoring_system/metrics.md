# Viral Scoring System

Every content idea generated by the Strategy Agent must be scored before moving to the Scripting phase.

## Score Definitions

| Metric | Range | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Viral Potential** | 1-10 | Score of 8+ required for "Hero" content. |
| **Originality** | 1-10 | Ensures we are not just copying competitors. |
| **Brand Fit** | 1-10 | Must be 10/10 to proceed. |
| **Complexity** | 1-10 | Lower is better for production efficiency. |

## Thresholds for Approval

- **Green Light**: Total score > 32/40. Proceed to full production.
- **Yellow Light**: Total score 25-31. Requires refinement of the hook or educational angle.
- **Red Light**: Total score < 25. Archive the idea in the `memory/failed_content` folder.
