# Animation & Motion Prompt Library

## Motion Principles

For YouTube Kids, motion must be **Rhythmic**, **Predictable**, and **Expressive**. Avoid fast, chaotic movements that might overwhelm younger viewers.

## Multi-Shot Framework

Use these motion descriptions for the 5-shot method:

### 1. The Establish (Track)
> `Slow camera tracking, character moving into frame, environmental elements gently swaying (leaves, clouds).`

### 2. The Focus (Static/Low Angle)
> `Static camera, character stopping, head tilting, eyes blinking, ears twitching.`

### 3. The Detail (Push In)
> `Slow zoom/push in on character's hand or tool, subtle finger movements, glowing effects activating.`

### 4. The Action (Pan/Mimic)
> `Smooth panning following character movement, exaggerated but slow physical actions (swinging, hopping).`

### 5. The Resolution (Pull Out)
> `Wide camera pull out, multiple characters waving, background elements sparkling.`

## Tool-Specific Instructions

- **Runway Gen-3**: Use `Motion Score: 5` for gentle scenes, `Motion Score: 7` for space adventures.
- **Luma Dream Machine**: Emphasize `consistent character features` and `vibrant color preservation`.
