# Safety Policy

## Purpose

This document outlines the strict safety guidelines and compliance requirements for all content generated by the AI-YouTube-Kids-Production-OS. Its primary goal is to ensure that all videos are safe, age-appropriate, and adhere to regulatory standards like COPPA.

## Context

This policy is a critical component of the `core/` governance layer, guiding AI agents in content creation to prevent the generation of harmful, inappropriate, or non-compliant material. It safeguards the brand's reputation and ensures a secure viewing environment for children.

## Inputs

- **Content Rules**: `core/rules/content_rules.md` (for general content appropriateness).
- **Target Audience Age Group**: `knowledge/project/identity.md` (to tailor safety measures).

## Outputs

- **Safe Content**: Videos free from dangerous acts, harmful themes, or inappropriate language.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Adherence to standards like COPPA.

## Related Files

- `core/rules/content_rules.md`
- `knowledge/project/identity.md`

## Dependencies

- **QA Agent**: Primarily responsible for verifying compliance with this policy during content review.
- **All AI Agents**: Must implicitly follow these guidelines during content generation.

## COPPA Compliance

The system is designed to produce content that is "Made for Kids" and must comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). No personal data should be collected from the audience through the content itself.

## Visual Safety

Visuals must be reviewed for potential triggers. This includes ensuring that animations are smooth and do not contain rapid flickering. All character designs must be friendly and non-threatening.

## Narrative Safety

Stories must promote positive values such as kindness, sharing, and curiosity. Any conflict presented must be resolved in a constructive manner that children can easily understand.

| Safety Check | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No Dangerous Acts** | No depictions of characters performing stunts or risky behaviors. |
| **No Harmful Themes** | Avoidance of violence, scary imagery, or adult topics. |
| **Positive Reinforcement** | Focus on rewarding good behavior and learning. |
| **Parental Trust** | Content should be something a parent feels safe leaving their child with. |

## Examples

- **Visual Safety**: Ensuring that a character's fall is cartoonish and without injury, rather than realistic.
- **Narrative Safety**: A story about sharing toys resolves with characters happily playing together, not with conflict or sadness.
- **COPPA**: Avoiding any direct calls to action that ask children for personal information or to visit external websites without parental consent.
