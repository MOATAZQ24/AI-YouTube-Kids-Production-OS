# Production Standards

## Purpose

This document defines the technical and aesthetic quality benchmarks for all content generated by the AI-YouTube-Kids-Production-OS. Adherence to these standards ensures a consistent, high-quality viewer experience across all platforms.

## Context

These standards are part of the `core/` governance layer, providing concrete specifications for AI agents involved in asset generation, editing, and final output. They are crucial for maintaining brand professionalism and technical compatibility with platforms like YouTube Kids.

## Inputs

- **Brand Bible**: `knowledge/brand/bible.md` (for visual identity and storytelling style).
- **Character Bibles**: `knowledge/characters/` (for consistent character designs).

## Outputs

- **High-Quality Assets**: Images, animations, and audio files meeting specified resolutions, frame rates, and formats.
- **Consistent Output**: Videos that look and sound uniform across different episodes.

## Dependencies

- **Visual Agent**: Uses these standards for image and animation generation.
- **Voice Agent**: Adheres to audio quality standards.
- **QA Agent**: Verifies compliance with all production standards.

## Visual Quality

All images and animations must meet high-resolution standards (minimum 1080p). The visual style should be consistent across all episodes, using the approved color palette and character models.

## Audio Quality

Voiceovers must be clear, well-paced, and emotionally appropriate for the target age group. Background music should be upbeat but not overpowering, ensuring that the narration remains the primary focus.

## Technical Specifications

| Asset Type | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Video Resolution** | 1920x1080 (16:9) |
| **Frame Rate** | 24fps or 30fps |
| **Audio Format** | AAC, 128kbps+, Stereo |
| **File Format** | MP4 (H.264) |
| **Thumbnail Size** | 1280x720 |

## Examples

- **Visual Quality**: Ensuring all character models maintain their exact design specifications, including color codes and proportions, as defined in their respective character profiles.
- **Audio Quality**: A voiceover should be free of background noise, echoes, and sibilance, with a consistent volume level throughout the video.
- **Technical Specifications**: All final video files must be exported as MP4 (H.264) at 1920x1080 resolution and 24 frames per second to ensure compatibility and quality on YouTube Kids.
