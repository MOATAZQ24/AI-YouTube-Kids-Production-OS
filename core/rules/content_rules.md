# Content Rules

## Purpose

This document defines the fundamental guidelines and principles that govern all content produced by the AI-YouTube-Kids-Production-OS. Its primary goal is to ensure that all videos are educational, engaging, and appropriate for the target audience.

## Context

These rules are a critical component of the `core/` governance layer, providing a framework for AI agents to adhere to during content generation. They are designed to maintain brand integrity and audience trust.

## Inputs

- **Brand Bible**: `knowledge/brand/bible.md` (for tone, voice, and educational philosophy).
- **Target Audience Profile**: `knowledge/project/identity.md` (for age group and learning objectives).

## Outputs

- **Compliant Content**: Videos that meet educational, engagement, and safety standards.
- **Consistent Quality**: A baseline for all AI-generated narratives and visuals.

- **Strategy Agent**: Uses these rules during idea generation and validation.
- **QA Agent**: References these rules for content review and approval.

## Educational Value

Every piece of content must have a clear educational objective. Whether it's teaching colors, numbers, social skills, or scientific concepts, the "Learning Goal" must be defined before scripting begins.

## Engagement Standards

Content must balance education with entertainment. The "Viral Intelligence Engine" should be used to ensure hooks are effective within the first 5 seconds to maximize retention.

## Safety and Compliance

All content must strictly adhere to the YouTube Kids safety guidelines. This includes avoiding any depictions of dangerous activities, inappropriate language, or sensitive themes that are not age-appropriate.

| Rule Category | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Language** | Simple, clear, and positive. Avoid slang or complex jargon. |
| **Visuals** | Bright, high-contrast colors. No flashing lights that could trigger seizures. |
| **Pacing** | Moderate speed. Fast enough to hold attention, slow enough to be understood. |
| **Call to Action** | Encouraging and interactive (e.g., "Can you find the red ball?"). |

## Examples

- **Educational Goal**: A video about shapes might focus on identifying circles, squares, and triangles in everyday objects.
- **Engagement**: A catchy song about counting from 1 to 10, with visual cues for each number.
- **Safety**: Ensuring that characters always wear helmets when riding bikes, even in animated scenarios.
