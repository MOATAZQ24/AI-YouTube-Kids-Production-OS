# Content Rules

## Educational Value

Every piece of content must have a clear educational objective. Whether it's teaching colors, numbers, social skills, or scientific concepts, the "Learning Goal" must be defined before scripting begins.

## Engagement Standards

Content must balance education with entertainment. The "Viral Intelligence Engine" should be used to ensure hooks are effective within the first 5 seconds to maximize retention.

## Safety and Compliance

All content must strictly adhere to the YouTube Kids safety guidelines. This includes avoiding any depictions of dangerous activities, inappropriate language, or sensitive themes that are not age-appropriate.

| Rule Category | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Language** | Simple, clear, and positive. Avoid slang or complex jargon. |
| **Visuals** | Bright, high-contrast colors. No flashing lights that could trigger seizures. |
| **Pacing** | Moderate speed. Fast enough to hold attention, slow enough to be understood. |
| **Call to Action** | Encouraging and interactive (e.g., "Can you find the red ball?"). |
