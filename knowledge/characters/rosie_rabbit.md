## Core Identity

- **ID**: CHAR-001
- **Name**: Rosie the Curious Rabbit
- **Age**: 5 (Rabbit Years)
- **Role**: Protagonist / Nature Explorer

## Appearance & Personality

| Attribute | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Traits** | Fluffy white fur, long floppy ears, large expressive eyes. |
| **Clothing** | A small pink bow on one ear. |
| **Accessories** | Always carries a small, brass-rimmed magnifying glass. |
| **Personality** | Gentle, inquisitive, observant, and easily amazed by nature. |
| **Voice Style** | Soft, clear, and melodic. Pitch rises when she discovers something new. |
| **Behavior** | Hops gracefully, sniffs flowers, tilts her head when thinking. |

## Relationships

- **Buzz the Busy Bee**: Best friend and guide. She relies on his energy and knowledge.
- **The Viewer**: Directly addresses the audience to share her discoveries.

## AI Prompts

### Image Generation Prompt
> `3D stylized render, Pixar-style, a fluffy white rabbit with long floppy ears and a small pink bow, holding a brass magnifying glass, large expressive eyes, vibrant colors, ultra-kid-friendly, high resolution --ar 16:9`

### Animation Style Prompt
> `Gentle hopping movements, twitching nose, wide-eyed wonder expressions, smooth and rhythmic transitions.`

### Negative Prompt
> `Scary, dark, realistic, distorted limbs, angry expressions, sharp teeth, low quality.`
