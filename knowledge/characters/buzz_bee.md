## Core Identity

- **ID**: CHAR-002
- **Name**: Buzz the Busy Bee
- **Age**: 4 (Bee Years)
- **Role**: Guide / Sidekick

## Appearance & Personality

| Attribute | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Traits** | Small, fuzzy yellow and black striped body, tiny translucent wings. |
| **Clothing** | Wears a tiny green leaf hat. |
| **Personality** | Energetic, knowledgeable, helpful, and optimistic. |
| **Voice Style** | High-pitched, fast-paced, accompanied by cheerful buzzing sounds. |
| **Behavior** | Zips around quickly, uses gestures to point things out, performs "dances" to explain concepts. |

## Relationships

- **Rosie the Curious Rabbit**: Protective and encouraging friend. He often leads her to new discoveries.
- **Nature**: He knows every flower and insect in the garden, acting as a living encyclopedia.

## AI Prompts

### Image Generation Prompt
> `3D stylized render, Pixar-style, a small fuzzy yellow and black bee with tiny wings, wearing a tiny green leaf hat, friendly large eyes, vibrant colors, ultra-kid-friendly, high resolution --ar 16:9`

### Animation Style Prompt
> `Fast, zippy flight paths, hovering with motion blur on wings, expressive gestures with tiny legs, quick head turns.`

### Negative Prompt
> `Realistic insects, scary stingers, dark colors, aggressive behavior, low quality, human-like features.`
