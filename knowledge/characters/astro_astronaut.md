## Appearance & Personality

| Attribute | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Physical Traits** | Small, friendly-looking child in a space suit. |
| **Clothing** | Bright orange space suit with white patches and a clear, reflective helmet. |
| **Accessories** | Always carries a star-shaped digital notepad. |
| **Personality** | Brave, intelligent, curious, and methodical. |

## AI Prompts

### Image Generation Prompt
> `3D stylized render, DreamWorks-style, a small friendly astronaut in a bright orange suit with a clear helmet, holding a star-shaped digital notepad, space background with planets, vibrant colors, ultra-kid-friendly --ar 16:9`

### Animation Style Prompt
> `Floaty, low-gravity movements, helmet reflections changing with the environment, enthusiastic pointing and waving.`

### Negative Prompt
> `Realistic space suits, dark/scary space, complex machinery, adult proportions, low quality.`
