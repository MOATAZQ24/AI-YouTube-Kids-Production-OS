# Project Identity

## Mission

To create high-quality, AI-powered educational content that inspires curiosity and learning in children worldwide.

## Vision

To become the leading digital production company for YouTube Kids, leveraging cutting-edge AI to deliver consistent, engaging, and safe content at scale.

## Audience Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Age Group** | 3-7 years old |
| **Secondary Age Group** | Parents and educators |
| **Languages** | English (Primary), Spanish, French, Mandarin |
| **Target Regions** | Global, with focus on North America, Europe, and Asia |

## Brand Positioning

The brand is positioned as a "Trusted Learning Companion," combining the magic of storytelling with structured educational outcomes.
