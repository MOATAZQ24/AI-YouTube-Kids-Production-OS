# Project Identity

## Purpose

This document defines the core identity and strategic positioning of the AI-YouTube-Kids-Production-OS. It articulates the mission, vision, target audience, and brand positioning to guide all content creation and strategic decisions.

## Context

As a foundational document within the `knowledge/project/` directory, the Project Identity ensures that all AI agents and human collaborators operate with a unified understanding of the brand's goals and target demographic. It is essential for maintaining consistency in content themes and educational objectives.

## Inputs

- **Market Research**: Analysis of the YouTube Kids landscape and competitor offerings.
- **Educational Goals**: Desired learning outcomes for the target age group.

## Outputs

- **Strategic Direction**: Clear guidelines for content development and brand messaging.
- **Audience Focus**: Defined demographic for all content targeting.

## Related Files

- `knowledge/brand/bible.md`

## Dependencies

- **Strategy Agent**: Uses this document to align content ideas with the project's mission and audience.
- **QA Agent**: References audience and safety guidelines during content review.

## Mission

To create high-quality, AI-powered educational content that inspires curiosity and learning in children worldwide.

## Vision

To become the leading digital production company for YouTube Kids, leveraging cutting-edge AI to deliver consistent, engaging, and safe content at scale.

## Audience Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Age Group** | 3-7 years old |
| **Secondary Age Group** | Parents and educators |
| **Languages** | English (Primary), Spanish, French, Mandarin |
| **Target Regions** | Global, with focus on North America, Europe, and Asia |

## Brand Positioning

The brand is positioned as a "Trusted Learning Companion," combining the magic of storytelling with structured educational outcomes.

## Examples

- **Mission in Action**: Producing a series of videos that teach basic physics concepts through engaging stories about friendly robots.
- **Audience Focus**: Developing content with simple language and bright visuals specifically designed to capture the attention of 3-7 year olds.
- **Brand Positioning**: Creating a video where a character gently guides children through a learning process, reinforcing positive values and curiosity.
