# Story Bible: Narrative Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the core narrative structures, storytelling principles, and character interaction guidelines for the AI-YouTube-Kids-Production-OS. Its purpose is to ensure consistent, engaging, and educationally sound storytelling across all content.

## Context

As a central knowledge asset within the `knowledge/stories/` directory, the Story Bible provides the narrative blueprint for the Writer Agent and other AI agents. It ensures that all generated stories adhere to established plot devices, character arcs, and thematic elements.

- **Project Identity**: `knowledge/project/identity.md` (for audience and educational goals).

- **Character Bibles**: `knowledge/characters/` (for character personalities and relationships).

## Outputs

- **Educational Integration**: Seamless embedding of learning objectives within the story.

## The 5-Shot Cinematic Method

Every episode or segment follows a structured visual narrative to ensure high engagement and production efficiency.

### Shot Breakdown
1. **Shot 1: The Hook (Wide)** - Establish the setting and the character's immediate curiosity.
2. **Shot 2: The Observation (Medium)** - The character identifies a specific element to explore.
3. **Shot 3: The Discovery (Close-up)** - A moment of wonder or interaction with a detail.
4. **Shot 4: The Interaction (Medium)** - The character acts upon their discovery (mimicking, nibbling, etc.).
5. **Shot 5: The Integration (Wide)** - A resolution where the discovery is shared or celebrated.

## Recurring Themes

| Theme | Narrative Goal |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Curiosity** | Encouraging viewers to ask "What's that?" |
| **Friendship** | Showing characters helping each other or the viewer. |
| **Perseverance** | Characters trying something new, even if it's awkward at first. |
| **Wonder** | Finding magic in everyday things (sand, carrots, stars). |

## Dialogue Guidelines

- **Rosie**: Uses "I wonder..." and "Oh my!"
- **Buzz**: Uses "Buzz, buzz!" and "Look at this!"
- **Astro**: Uses "Wow, Nova!" and "Let's investigate."
- **Nova**: Uses "Indeed, Astro!" and "Did you know...?"

## Examples

- **5-Shot Method**: In a story about Rosie discovering a new flower, Shot 1 shows her in a meadow, Shot 2 focuses on her noticing a unique flower, Shot 3 is a close-up of her magnifying glass on the flower, Shot 4 shows her gently touching it, and Shot 5 shows her happily sharing her discovery with Buzz.
- **Educational Integration**: A story about sharing (Perseverance theme) might involve two characters learning to take turns with a toy, with the educational lesson being the importance of cooperation.
