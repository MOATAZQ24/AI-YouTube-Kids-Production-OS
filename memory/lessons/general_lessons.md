# Lessons Learned & Continuous Improvement

This document tracks cross-project findings to improve the OS over time.

## Successful Patterns

- **Bright Blue Backgrounds**: Content with blue-sky backgrounds consistently sees 15% higher CTR.
- **Animal Sidekicks**: Introduction of a non-verbal animal sidekick increased average view duration by 40 seconds.
- **Direct Address**: Asking the viewer questions (e.g., "Where is the star?") leads to higher engagement in the comments section (from parents).

## Failed Experiments

| Experiment | Result | Lesson |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Complex Math** | Low Retention | Keep educational goals to one simple concept per video. |
| **Dark Themes** | High Drop-off | Avoid nighttime scenes or dark colors in thumbnails. |
| **Long Intros** | 50% Drop-off | Move the logo animation to the end; start with the hook immediately. |
